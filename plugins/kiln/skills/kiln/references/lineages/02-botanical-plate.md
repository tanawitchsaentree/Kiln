# 02-botanical-plate — Botanical plate

## Conditions

One specimen, recorded as the authoritative single account of it, made for identification and
preservation rather than persuasion. There is no ambiguity about scale because a ruler or scale bar
is part of the convention, not an afterthought. A brief matches this lineage when its real problem
is a single authoritative record of one thing, not a comparison between many things that merely
look similar to it.

## Home vector

`C2 T6 G2 S3 M1 D1` — passes `scripts/check_vector.py` on its own. Type is the loud axis at 6,
because the plate's authority is carried by the caption, not by the drawing: a serif or fine display
face setting the Latin binomial with real typographic care is what tells the reader this is a
scientific record and not a garden illustration.

## Hierarchy logic

Isolation does the first job: the specimen floats alone on a plain ground with generous surrounding
space, so nothing competes with it for attention. A single strong caption hierarchy does the second:
the binomial name set in italic above a subordinate line of common name and notes, weight and style
contrast doing the ranking rather than size.

The same isolation is the separator, not a border or a shadow around the plate — space-led per
`references/foundations/depth.md`. Authority comes precisely from having nothing else in the frame
to draw a boundary against; a border around the specimen would imply it needs containing, when the
convention's whole claim is that the specimen is the entire content of the page and needs none.

## Colour logic

Three roles: a near-neutral ground, the specimen rendered in observed, accurate colour, and a single
thin neutral ruling for the scale marker. Colour here is documentary, not semantic — the governing
rule is fidelity to the specimen, never decoration or signal. Nothing is colour-coded because nothing
in the plate needs a reserved warning hue; the whole convention is about one accurate record.

## Rhythm

The interval is set by the margin ratio around the specimen and by the fixed distance from the base
of the specimen to the caption block. Where a scale bar is present, its own unit becomes the module
that the rest of the plate's proportions echo.

## Signature move

A single caption line, set with real typographic discipline, doing the entire identification job
with no supporting chrome around it. Strip the typographic care from that line and the plate becomes
a nature illustration with a name attached, not a botanical plate — the authority was never in the
drawing.

## What it hands the system for free

A ready single-record detail pattern: name plus a minimal structured caption, and generous isolation
as the default hierarchy device, so no extra container chrome has to be invented to make one record
read as authoritative.

## Type character

A serif or fine display face in a formal register, set rather than drawn, doing real work at display
size. Italic is load-bearing, not stylistic — it is the taxonomic convention. Numerals are lining
where they appear; there is little tabular content to demand otherwise.

## Voice

Formal and exact. Latin binomial names, then plain factual notes — habitat, collection date, scale —
with no adjectives and no persuasion. The register never warms up, because warmth was never the
point.

## Failure mode

Pushed into a context with many items needing comparison, the isolation logic has nothing to set the
specimen against and starts reading as merely empty. Pushed to higher density, it has no answer: the
lineage carries exactly one caption hierarchy and no list or table pattern, so a second record simply
duplicates the first plate rather than relating to it.

## What it cancels

Multi-column comparison layouts, saturated or symbolic colour, more than one level of caption
hierarchy, and any decorative flourish that competes with the specimen for attention.

## Behaviour pulled off home

Push grid or density up toward many specimens per view and isolation is the first thing to break —
the plate-per-specimen relationship this lineage depends on cannot compress into a grid without
losing the authority signal that isolation was carrying, which is the point at which the brief has
actually walked into seed-catalogue territory. Push type violence down further and the caption loses
its display-level care, and with it the one signal that told the reader this was an authoritative
record rather than a plain diagram.
