# 11-glaze-notebook — Glaze notebook

## Conditions

Personal record-keeping of formulas and recipes, written for the maker's own later use rather than
for a stranger. The notation is idiosyncratic and works precisely because only the maker has to
parse it — there is no reader to onboard, so the shorthand never had to become a convention.

## Home vector

`C3 T3 G2 S6 M1 D4` — passes `scripts/check_vector.py` (spread 5, no concentration or payment
issue). Surface is the loud axis at 6: the whole point of the notebook is recording material and
surface quality — how a glaze actually fired, its texture, its colour shift, whether it crawled or
crackled. The surface itself is the content being recorded, not a finish applied to some other
content.

## Hierarchy logic

Not size, but the fired-result swatch as anchor: the actual test tile, or a description of its
surface, sits directly against its recipe, and the eye checks the result before it checks the
formula that produced it. The second device is marginal annotation added in a different hand or ink
at the page edge — because corrections happen later, that later layer visually interrupts the
original entry, and the interruption itself is the hierarchy: newest note reads first.

## Colour logic

Two roles: a plain ground and one ink for the recorded formula. Colour is not spent as an interface
device here, because the glaze's actual fired colour is the informational payload, carried by the
swatch or the photograph rather than by any styling choice the notebook itself makes. The system
stays quiet on colour so the material's own colour can be the thing being read.

## Rhythm

The repeating unit is the recipe block — formula, firing note, and result, in that order — but its
height is not fixed, since it's set by however many ingredients a given formula needs. The rhythm is
the recurring three-part structure repeating down the page, not a fixed spacing module.

## Signature move

The fired-result swatch sitting directly against its recipe, so a formula is never read without its
outcome beside it. Remove the swatch and the page becomes a plain ingredient list, indistinguishable
from a shopping list and missing the entire reason the notebook exists.

## What it hands the system for free

A built-in way to show "the same thing, before and after a process" without inventing a
before/after comparison pattern from scratch. It also hands tolerance for a private, non-standardized
shorthand — abbreviations and personal symbols a stranger-facing system could never get away with,
because there's no first-time reader here to fail.

## Type character

Handmade register for the annotations — drawn, abbreviated, specific to the maker — with tabular
numerals for measurements, percentages, and cone numbers, since those are the one thing actually
compared across entries. The voice can look inconsistent page to page, because it was never meant to
be read by anyone but its author.

## Voice

Private and abbreviated, plain to the point of cryptic. The shorthand is addressed to a future self
who already has the context, not to a first-time reader, so it can skip explanation entirely.
Warmth doesn't apply; this is a note-to-self, not an announcement.

## Failure mode

Pushed onto a multi-user or public surface, the private shorthand that makes it fast for the maker
becomes illegible to everyone else, and the notebook's whole logic — no onboarding needed because
there's only one reader — actively works against a surface that has to be readable by strangers on
first use.

## What it cancels

Cancels standardized, universally legible iconography; the notation is personal, not a convention
meant to travel. Cancels a polished presentation register — this is a working note, not a report.
Cancels a first-time navigation architecture built for someone encountering the system cold.

## Behaviour pulled off home

Surface is the one axis this lineage cannot go quiet on without ceasing to be this lineage — pull it
down toward the low end and there's no fired-result quality left to record, and the whole tradition
loses its reason for existing. Pulled the other way, toward higher density, the marginal-annotation
device breaks first, since there's no room left at the page edge for a later correction to interrupt
the original entry.
