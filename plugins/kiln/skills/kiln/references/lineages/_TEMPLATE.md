# {NN}-{slug} — {Name}

Do not fill this in by guessing at the medium's look. Every field is a claim about how the medium
actually solved its own problem, and a field with nothing real to say is more honest left short than
padded.

## Conditions

The kind of problem this tradition was built to solve in its original medium, stated as a
condition a brief can be checked against — not as a mood or a list of adjectives. Two or three
sentences. A brief matches this lineage when its actual problem resembles this one, not when its
subject matter is superficially adjacent.

## Home vector

`C{n} T{n} G{n} S{n} M{n} D{n}` — the vector this tradition sits at when translated into an
interface with no brief-specific pull in either direction. Must pass `scripts/check_vector.py` on
its own. State in one line which axis is the lineage's natural loud one and why the medium itself
was loud there.

## Hierarchy logic

What tells the eye what matters first, in this tradition, when it is not size. Position, enclosure,
repetition, isolation, colour reserved for one thing, weight. Name the specific device or two this
tradition actually uses — most traditions lean on two, rarely three.

## Colour logic

How many roles exist and what governs them — not which hues. State the number of roles (ground,
structure, one reserved signal, etc.) and the rule that governs the reserved one, if there is one.

## Rhythm

The interval that repeats in this tradition, and what generates it — a grid module, a baseline, a
component's own recurring measurement. State it as a ratio or a repeatable rule, not as "regular
spacing."

## Signature move

The one thing that, if removed, makes an interface built in this lineage stop looking like it
belongs to the lineage. Specific and checkable — Gate G2 of the coherence set tests for this move
being present and doing real work, so vague answers here make that gate unrunnable.

## What it hands the system for free

What an interface gets automatically by adopting this lineage, without having to invent it — a
hierarchy device, a spacing logic, a state-communication method the tradition already solved. This
is the payoff for choosing a lineage instead of defaulting.

## Type character

Not a typeface recommendation. The character the type needs to carry — geometric or humanist, set
or drawn, formal or handmade register, whether numerals are tabular, whether display sizes do real
work or type stays flat and lets position carry hierarchy (per `references/foundations/tokens.md`'s
sibling concern in `intensity.md`'s T axis).

## Voice

One or two sentences on register, per `references/voice.md`. Where this tradition's actual language
(labels, captions, warnings, the words printed alongside the marks) sits between formal and plain,
warm and neutral.

## Failure mode

What goes wrong when this lineage is pushed somewhere it does not fit, or pushed too far from its
home vector. Specific enough to recognise in a real build, not a generic warning.

## What it cancels

The defaults this lineage rules out by design — a specific colour habit, a type habit, a spacing
habit that the tradition's own logic makes wrong, not just unlikely.

## Behaviour pulled off home

How this lineage's signature move and hierarchy logic degrade or shift as a controlling axis moves
far from the home vector stated above — what survives the pull, and what breaks first. This is what
lets a brief pull a lineage toward its own version of the tradition without silently becoming a
different lineage or silently becoming the baseline.
