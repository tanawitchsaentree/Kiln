# 10-field-guide — Field guide

## Conditions

Rapid identification in the field, under time pressure, where the reader has seconds to decide
which of several near-identical things they're looking at. The book has to be portable and the
comparison has to work on a small number of diagnostic marks — this bird against that very similar
bird — usually shown on a plate that puts several confusable species side by side so the difference
is visible rather than described from memory.

## Home vector

`C4 T5 G3 S3 M1 D6` — passes `scripts/check_vector.py` (spread 5, no concentration or payment
issue). No axis reaches the 7 that `intensity.md` and the gate files treat as the threshold for
"loud." This is a genuinely quiet system at home: nothing here is spent boldly, because the medium's
confidence comes from precision, per `gates-precision.md`, not from a spent loud axis. That's a
legitimate profile as long as spread still clears 5, which it does.

## Hierarchy logic

Not size, but isolation of the diagnostic mark — a small callout, arrow, or circled detail pointing
to the one feature that separates this entry from its most-confusable neighbour. The second device
is plate consistency: every species on a comparison plate is drawn at the same pose, same scale, and
same orientation, so the only thing that differs across the plate is the thing the reader actually
needs to see.

## Colour logic

Three roles: a plain ground, a structural ink for line work and captions, and colour reserved
strictly for identification — the plumage, petal, or cap colour is recorded because it is itself
diagnostic information, never applied as decoration or category-coding. If a colour on the page
doesn't help tell one entry from another, it shouldn't be there.

## Rhythm

The repeating unit is the plate module: a fixed ratio of illustration to caption, repeated at every
entry so the eye's scan path — look at the drawing, find the callout, read the caption — is
identical from species to species regardless of how much text a given entry needs.

## Signature move

The diagnostic callout: one small pointer isolating the exact feature that distinguishes an entry
from its nearest look-alike, present on every comparison plate. Remove it and the plate becomes a
plain illustration the reader has to do all the comparison work on unaided, which defeats the whole
point of a field guide.

## What it hands the system for free

A ready-made way to say "this is what to look for" without inventing a highlighting convention from
scratch, and a comparison layout built specifically for near-duplicate items — most systems present
two similar options identically and leave the reader to spot the difference; this lineage already
solved that.

## Type character

Set and plain, humanist and legible at small size, because field guides are small books meant to be
read outdoors, not display pieces. Captions carry exact diagnostic language — "bill: black,
straight," not persuasive description. Numerals are tabular wherever measurements are given so they
compare cleanly across entries.

## Voice

Precise and plain, in short diagnostic phrases, confident without hedging — "distinguished by," not
"may possibly be." The voice is doing the work a loud axis would otherwise do: since nothing here is
visually spent boldly, the exactness of the claim is the entire argument, per `gates-precision.md`.

## Failure mode

Pushed toward an identity or marketing surface, the quiet precision reads as flat or unfinished,
because there is no loud axis to give a first-time viewer a place to look — a field guide screen used
as a landing page has nothing built to be a focal moment. Pushed to a density well past home, the
diagnostic callouts start to overlap or crowd against the illustration itself, and the one mechanism
this lineage depends on becomes indistinguishable from the clutter it's supposed to cut through.

## What it cancels

Cancels a designed hero or focal moment — there's no loud axis budgeted for one. Cancels decorative
colour variety; every colour on the page has to be answerable to "does this help identify the
thing." Cancels a persuasive or mood-setting voice in favour of checkable, exact claims.

## Behaviour pulled off home

Plate consistency survives most pulls, since it costs nothing to keep even as density rises — it can
hold at a smaller scale as long as every entry shrinks uniformly. What breaks first if this lineage
is pulled toward a louder vector to serve a marketing need is the colour-reserved-for-identification
rule: introducing a decorative brand colour undermines the reader's trust that colour always means
something checkable, and once that trust breaks the diagnostic callout loses its authority too.
