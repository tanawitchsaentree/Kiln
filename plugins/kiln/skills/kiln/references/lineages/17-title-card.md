# 17-title-card — Title card

## Conditions

A single moment needs to land at maximum impact and then be gone. The content is a held frame, not
a screen someone operates — a name, a word, seen once for a few seconds and never referred back to.
There is no persistent chrome or navigation because there is nothing to navigate to; the entire
mechanism depends on brevity and on never being seen twice. Match this lineage when the brief's real
problem is a single moment of maximum impact with no persistence requirement, not any screen that
merely opens a flow.

## Home vector

`C6 T9 G2 S3 M7 D0` — two axes at 7 or above, the concentration limit. Type at 9 is the primary
loud axis and is extreme, paid for by grid at 2 and density at 0, both comfortably at or below the
payment threshold. Motion at 7 is the secondary loud axis: a title card is not a static poster, it
is a held frame with a specific, timed entrance and exit — the appear, the hold, the release — and
that timing is as much the medium's actual content as the type itself. A title card whose type just
appears with no timed entrance is a poster, `05-record-sleeve`'s static-object problem, not this
lineage's held-moment problem. Passes `check_vector.py`.

Earlier drafts of this file held motion at 4, treating the entrance timing as a supporting detail
of the type rather than a loud decision in its own right. That undersold the medium: film and
broadcast title cards are defined as much by the choreography of appearance (a hard cut versus a
slow fade-up versus a hold-then-snap) as by the type choice, and the two together are what makes a
title card read as a *moment* rather than a printed word on a screen.

## Hierarchy logic

Hierarchy here is not between multiple elements, because there is usually only one. It is between
that one held piece of type and the emptiness around it. The devices are isolation — one word alone
in a vast field with nothing competing for attention — and the scale and weight of that single
held instance, which the low grid value keeps simple: centred or otherwise plainly composed, never
elaborated.

Space-led per `references/foundations/depth.md`, in the most extreme form the three space-led
lineages take: there is nothing else on screen to separate the type from, so no border or shadow
mechanism is even relevant. The emptiness itself is the entire separating device, and it is what
makes this lineage the reference case for how confident space-led separation can be when nothing
competes with it.

## Colour logic

Two roles, sometimes near-monochrome: one figure colour for the type, one ground colour behind it.
No third role is needed because there is only ever one piece of content on screen at a time. Chroma
at 6 means the figure colour is present and can carry real weight, but it is spent on one thing, not
distributed across a palette.

## Rhythm

Not a spatial rhythm, since density sits at 0 — nothing repeats within a single card. The rhythm is
temporal: a fixed hold duration that repeats card to card across a sequence, such as three or four
seconds per name in an opening-credits run. That fixed interval, not any spacing module, is what
generates the sequence's actual pace.

## Signature move

A single word or name held at a scale and duration that gives it more weight than it would ever
carry sharing a screen with anything else. Remove the isolation or cut the hold time short and it
stops being a title card — it becomes a caption, because drama here comes entirely from scale plus
timing, never from decoration.

## What it hands the system for free

A solved pattern for establishing identity at maximum intensity in the shortest possible exposure —
useful anywhere a system needs a genuine first-impression moment: a splash, a launch screen, a
single held announcement, all inherit this move instead of inventing their own "big moment" from
scratch.

## Type character

Extreme and drawn or tightly set, with scale as the entire mechanism rather than a supporting lever.
This is not a lineage where type stays flat and position carries hierarchy; here type is the
hierarchy, and display size does the actual dramatic work the rest of the system might otherwise
spread across colour or motion.

## Voice

Terse to the point of gnomic. A name, a single word, no supporting sentence, because there is no
second beat in which to explain anything — whatever is said has to be complete in the one word it
gets.

## Failure mode

Per `references/constraint.md`, this lineage is identity-strong and application-weak, and it fails
hard the moment it has to hold a persistent UI, a form, or any repeated content: its entire mechanism
depends on being seen once, at maximum intensity, for a few seconds, and asking it to persist or to
be referred back to collapses the isolation and timing that made it work in the first place.

## What it cancels

Cancels persistent navigation or chrome entirely. Cancels the idea of a user returning to or
re-reading the same view, since there is no history and no state to return to. Cancels multiple
simultaneous focal points, and cancels tabular or dense content outright — density at 0 is not
incidental, it is the condition the whole lineage depends on.

## Behaviour pulled off home

The moment a brief pulls this toward persistence — density rising because content needs to stay on
screen, or grid rising because more than one thing needs to coexist — the lineage's logic fails
first and fastest, since isolation and a fixed hold time cannot survive being asked to do ongoing
work. Chroma and type violence can both move somewhat without breaking the lineage, because the
single-moment structure survives changes in how loud that one moment is, just not changes in how
long it is asked to last. Type and motion are already sharing the concentration ceiling at home, so
a brief that wants a third loud axis (more colour, more grid asymmetry) has to pull one of these two
down first — pulling type down toward a plainer face still leaves a title card if the timing holds;
pulling motion down toward an instant appear-with-no-transition is the riskier direction, since it
removes the held-moment quality (§ Rhythm) that separates this lineage from a static poster.
