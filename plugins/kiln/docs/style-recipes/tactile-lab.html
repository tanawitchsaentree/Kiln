Material & mechanism · Bauhaus panel

The same four mechanisms in three materials. Shade comes from one light at the top left. Movement is a spring whose mass and damping belong to the material: a press goes down in about 60ms and comes back on the spring, and the rotary and the fader settle into their detents. Try the keyboard too.

A · Enamel on steel

Light and glossy, a short throw. Springs are stiff and well damped (ζ ≈ 0.83), so it settles in about 200ms with no bounce.

B · Bakelite

Heavy and matte, a deep throw. More mass and softer springs (ζ ≈ 0.82): the same press takes longer to come back, as a heavy part would.

C · Print (flat Bauhaus)

No gloss and no blur, an offset ink shadow. The springs are very stiff (ζ = 1), so parts click into place like a registration mark.

Usability stays the same in every material. Labels are at least 4.5:1, every target is 44px or more, the rotary and the fader are sliders with arrow keys, and every control has its focus ring. Under Reduce motion nothing is thrown, and the detents fade instead of travelling.
