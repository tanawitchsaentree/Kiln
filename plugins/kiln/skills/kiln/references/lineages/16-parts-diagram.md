# 16-parts-diagram — Parts diagram

## Conditions

A whole needs to be understood as a set of discrete parts, each one related to the whole by an
explicit, checkable correspondence — a connecting line, a number, both — with no ambiguity about
what attaches where. There is no room for styling or decoration to carry meaning, because meaning
here is entirely structural: this part, that position, this label. Match this lineage when the
brief's real problem is unambiguous part-to-whole correspondence, not any diagram that merely looks
technical.

## Home vector

`C1 T2 G1 S1 M1 D6` — no axis reaches 7, so there is no loud axis at home position, and none is
needed. Spread passes at 5 (max 6, min 1). Everything here is deliberately quiet; density at 6 is
moderate rather than loud, reflecting that a real exploded diagram carries many parts and many
labels without a single one of them getting decorative treatment. The lineage's whole identity is
structural precision at zero decoration. Passes `check_vector.py`.

## Hierarchy logic

Hierarchy is not established by enclosure, colour, or scale, it is established by the explicit
connecting line and the sequential number attached to it — the correspondence itself is the
hierarchy device. Per `references/foundations/depth.md`, this is a border-led lineage: hairlines
separate parts and callouts, elevation is absent, because a shadow would suggest one part matters
more than another when in fact every part gets equal visual weight and only its number and position
in the explosion distinguish it.

## Colour logic

At most two roles. Monochrome line art on a neutral ground is the default, and where a second role
exists it is a single reserved colour marking the one part active in the current instruction step —
never used to differentiate parts generally, only to flag "this one, right now." Colour is never
identity here; it is a pointer.

## Rhythm

The interval is the offset between exploded parts along the assembly axis, spaced in proportion to
each part's own bounding size rather than to a fixed grid module. The rule is mechanical: parts are
spaced exactly far enough apart that their connecting lines never cross, which means the rhythm is
generated by the assembly's own geometry, not by a page grid imposed on top of it.

## Signature move

An explicit line running from a floating part to its exact point of attachment, carrying a number
that appears exactly once elsewhere — in a parts list, in a previous step. Remove the line-to-number
correspondence and what is left is a scattered collection of parts with no relationship between
them, which defeats the entire purpose of an exploded view.

## What it hands the system for free

A solved pattern for showing how an isolated piece maps to its place in a larger whole, usable
anywhere a system needs to show a control's, a component's, or a setting's relationship to the
larger structure it belongs to — wiring, dependency, or configuration relationships inherit this
move instead of inventing their own callout convention.

## Type character

Plain and geometric at a single weight. Numerals do labelling work only, set tabular where a parts
list appears, and no display size ever does dramatic work — position and the connecting line carry
all the meaning that would elsewhere be carried by typographic contrast.

## Voice

Plain and imperative in instructional contexts — "Insert part 4 into slot A" — otherwise purely
nominal: a part name and a number, nothing evaluative, no adjectives, no marketing language
anywhere near a part's label.

## Failure mode

This lineage is border-led and application-strong, and it fails hard the moment it is asked to carry
identity or marketing work: every part looks like every other manufacturer's part because the entire
system deliberately withholds warmth, mood, and brand differentiation in favour of correspondence.
It also fails the moment colour or decoration creeps onto an individual part for emphasis, since that
breaks the equal-weight convention every connector line depends on to stay unambiguous.

## What it cancels

Cancels shadow or elevation as a separator in favour of hairlines only. Cancels colour as an
identity signal. Cancels display type doing expressive work. Cancels any illustrative embellishment
applied to a part for its own sake, since embellishment is indistinguishable from meaning in a
system this quiet.

## Behaviour pulled off home

Pull density higher — more parts, more steps — and the hairline separation and numbering scheme must
hold or the diagram stops being readable; the connector-to-number correspondence is the last device
to break, everything else compresses first. Pull chroma or type violence up and the lineage stops
being this one, since its entire value proposition is that nothing but structure is doing the work.
