# Phase 0 — Intake

Up to three adaptive questions, plus the delivery scale. Never a colour, never a font — those are
Phase 1 and Phase 2's decisions, made from a lineage and a vector, not from a preference stated cold
before either exists.

## The three questions

Adaptive, not scripted. Ask only what the brief hasn't already answered, and stop at three even if
more would help — a fourth question costs more in momentum than it returns in precision, and Phase
1's lineage pick and Phase 3's reference (if one exists) will surface most of what a fourth question
would have asked anyway.

Useful territory, pick from it rather than asking all of it: who uses this and under what pressure
(glancing, searching, deciding, monitoring), what happens when it's wrong (a missed reading costs
nothing versus costs a lot), whether a reference exists to study, whether a brand identity is
already fixed, and roughly how many distinct screens or components the brief actually needs.

Do not ask about aesthetic preference directly — "what look do you want" produces an answer stated
in web-default vocabulary before the lineage mechanism has had a chance to work. Ask about the
problem instead, and let the lineage pick in Phase 1 answer the aesthetic question as a consequence.

## Scale

Decide Spec, Package, or Program per `references/scale.md`, and state it in the first reply along
with a one-line reminder of that scale's honest limit. Do not let the scale decision hide inside a
longer answer — a user choosing Package scale needs to know now that it means one component per
session, not a full library by the end of this one.

If the brief's own language mentions a team, contribution, versioning, or a release cadence, that is
Program scale asking to happen under a different name — name the governance layer's limits from
`references/program.md` in the same reply, not after drafting has started.

## What to carry forward

The brief as understood after the three questions, the stated scale, and whether a reference exists
(if so, Phase 3 handles it — don't extract from it here). Nothing else. Phase 1 does not need to
know Phase 0's discarded question candidates.
