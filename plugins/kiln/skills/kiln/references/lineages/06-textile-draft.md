# 06-textile-draft — Textile draft

## Conditions

Repetition and pattern, where a structure repeats at every scale — the repeat unit itself, multiples
of it, and the whole cloth — and the same structural logic governs the smallest detail and the
largest layout. This is a weaving draft, a pattern notation, not a photograph of woven cloth. A
brief matches this lineage when its content genuinely recurs at more than one scale under one rule,
not when it merely looks textured.

## Home vector

`C6 T4 G7 S7 M2 D4` — passes `scripts/check_vector.py` on its own. Grid is the loud axis at 7,
because a woven structure notation is fundamentally a repeating grid system: warp and weft, a repeat
unit that tiles, is not a metaphor borrowed for this lineage, it is the actual notation the medium
uses.

## Hierarchy logic

Nested repeat does the first job: the same unit pattern recurs at multiple scales — thread, block,
whole cloth — and a part's position within the repeat, not its size, tells the eye what role it
plays. Notation-by-position does the second: a draft is read by grid-cell occupancy, a filled cell
meaning warp up, an empty one meaning warp down, never by colour or size.

## Colour logic

Two systems that vary independently. The structural notation itself runs on a small fixed value set,
often binary — mark or no mark, up or down. A separate colourway layer carries the actual thread
colours and can change freely without touching the structural notation. The governing rule is that
decoupling: structure and colour never merge into one signal.

## Rhythm

The repeat unit is the interval. A fixed thread count tiles both directions, and every larger
measurement in the piece is a whole-number multiple of that one repeat, never a fraction of it.

## Signature move

A single repeat unit that tiles losslessly at every scale, the same small structural block multiplied
without alteration to generate the whole layout from the smallest module up to the full composition.
Remove the requirement that everything be a whole-number multiple of one repeat, and it stops being
draft logic and becomes an arbitrary pattern with no structural claim behind it.

## What it hands the system for free

A solved fractal spacing and composition system: one repeat module legitimately governs spacing,
component sizing, and page-level layout under the same math, so "how big should this section be" and
"what is the spacing unit" are answered by the same rule instead of two unrelated ones.

## Type character

Geometric and structural, drawn from the grid itself rather than expressive. Numerals are tabular by
necessity, since thread counts and repeat counts are the actual content. Type stays fairly flat and
utilitarian, since the notation carries the visual interest, not typographic display.

## Voice

Technical and exact, an instructional-notation register: thread count, draw, weave structure, stated
plainly. There is no persuasion here — it is written for a maker executing the pattern, not for a
general reader.

## Failure mode

Pushed onto a one-off hero page or a single unique narrative screen with nothing that actually
recurs, the repeat-unit logic has nothing to tile and starts inventing artificial repetition just to
justify the grid, which reads as busywork rather than structure. Forcing the nested-repeat hierarchy
onto content that doesn't recur at multiple scales produces a pattern where none exists.

## What it cancels

One-off asymmetric hero compositions, a single dominant unrepeated gesture, spacing values that
aren't whole-number multiples of one module, and colour used as the primary structural signal —
structure and colour are deliberately kept separate here.

## Behaviour pulled off home

Pull grid down from 7 and the nested-repeat signature move is the first casualty: the lineage needs
the repeat unit visibly multiplying at more than one scale to read as itself, and a quieter grid
flattens it into a single-scale texture that looks like ordinary pattern rather than a structural
notation. Pull surface down far and the cloth-like material quality fades, but the notation logic
survives intact, since the repeat-unit math never depended on material rendering to function.
